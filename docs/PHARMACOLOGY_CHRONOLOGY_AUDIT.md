# Pharmacological contextualization chronology audit — Issue 27

## Integrity finding
The pharmacological contextualization and evidence-to-experiment triage were introduced during peer-review revision in response to the reviewer’s request for pharmacological interpretation. They were not part of the original molecular analysis plan.

The reviewer-response document itself provides the provenance: it states that the authors **added a separate pharmacological contextualization for all 12 Class A–C candidates**. Therefore, describing that same layer as **prespecified** is chronologically inaccurate.

## Corrected interpretation
The manuscript should describe this component as a **revision-added structured/descriptive pharmacological contextualization layer**. It is an interpretive evidence layer applied after the molecular candidate hierarchy had already been formed.

The correction does not change:

- TCGA-BRCA age-association results;
- GSE42568 replication results;
- ER-stratified sensitivity results;
- Class A–D assignments;
- STRING/network analysis;
- GO/KEGG enrichment results;
- the 12 Class A–C candidates;
- pharmacological evidence categories for those candidates; or
- the membership of the three follow-up routes.

## Corrected wording
Methods: the 12 Class A–C candidates were **subjected during peer-review revision to a structured pharmacological contextualization step**. The layer is explicitly identified as **revision-added** and **not part of the original molecular analysis plan**, and is treated as descriptive evidence contextualization rather than as a prespecified validation endpoint.

Results: **Applying this revision-added follow-up interpretation** yielded the same three non-ordinal routes.

## Scope
This audit corrects chronology and claim provenance only. It does not retroactively characterize other analyses as post hoc and does not modify any quantitative output.
