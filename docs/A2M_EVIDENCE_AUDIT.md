# A2M evidence audit

## Scope

This audit addresses only the pharmacological/prognostic wording used for **A2M (alpha-2-macroglobulin)** in the manuscript's candidate-contextualization section.

## Evidence-supported statement

A2M has published breast-cancer expression and survival associations, but the available evidence does **not** justify presenting A2M as an established breast-cancer therapeutic target. The manuscript therefore retains A2M as **Contextual / exploratory** and describes the evidence as expression/prognostic association rather than therapeutic validation.

## Primary supporting study

- Olbromski M, Mrozowska M, Piotrowska A, Kmiecik A, Smolarz B, Romanowicz H, Blasiak P, Maciejczyk A, Wojnar A, Dziegiel P (2024)
- *Prognostic significance of alpha-2-macrglobulin and low-density lipoprotein receptor-related protein-1 in various cancers*
- American Journal of Cancer Research 14(6):3036–3058
- PMID: **39005669**
- PMCID: **PMC11236788**
- DOI: **10.62347/VUJV9180**
- Breast-cancer arm: invasive ductal carcinoma; A2M expression was evaluated against clinicopathological features and overall survival.

## Supporting subtype/proteomics study

- Suman S, Basak T, Gupta P, Mishra S, Kumar V, Sengupta S, Shukla Y (2016)
- *Quantitative proteomics revealed novel proteins associated with molecular subtypes of breast cancer*
- Journal of Proteomics 148:183–193
- PMID: **27498393**
- DOI: **10.1016/j.jprot.2016.07.033**
- A2M was significantly modulated in triple-negative breast-cancer plasma/tissue and discussed as a subtype-associated diagnostic/prognostic signal.

## Claim boundary

Permitted manuscript wording:

> Published breast-cancer expression and survival associations; retained as exploratory rather than therapeutic evidence.

Not supported by the cited evidence:

- A2M is a clinically validated breast-cancer biomarker.
- A2M is an approved or established breast-cancer drug target.
- A2M-directed therapy improves breast-cancer outcomes.
- The present age-expression analysis predicts treatment response through A2M.

This audit changes citation support and wording precision only. It does not change any molecular, network, enrichment, benchmark, controller, comparator, ablation, or figure result.
