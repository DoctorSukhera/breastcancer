# Data availability and limitations

**Included:** frozen constructed 760-scenario benchmark inputs and reference policy; action registry and goals; benchmark trajectories/summary tables; molecular aggregate/selected-candidate source tables; pharmacology audit tables; source code for TCGA/GEO/STRING/GO-KEGG workflows; plotted figures.

**Not included:** raw/patient-level TCGA-BRCA expression and clinical records, GSE42568 expression matrix/platform data, original STRING retrieval outputs, historical GO/KEGG gene-set releases and complete enrichment background/query inputs. Original external datasets must be obtained from their providers in accordance with their terms.

**Reproducibility boundary:** the internal constructed benchmark is executable, but internal policy conformance is not an externally validated estimate of controller generalizability. External gene replication is a directional correlation, not a matched adjusted effect-size estimate. Historical GO/KEGG significance and clustering counts require source provenance verification. The candidate classifications are hypothesis-generating, not validated biomarkers or drug targets.

**Privacy:** do not commit confidential reviewer correspondence, patient identifiers, authorization tokens, or controlled-access biological data.
