Figure 2. Age-associated breast-cancer candidate evidence from adjusted discovery, external replication and receptor-stratified sensitivity. (A) HC3-robust adjusted age coefficients and 95% confidence intervals for 12 selected genes in TCGA-BRCA, with BH-adjusted q-values. (B) Signed Spearman correlations with age in the independent GSE42568 cohort; colored diamonds indicate significant directional replication (BH-FDR < 0.05), and grey circles indicate nonsignificant directional replication. (C) ER-positive and ER-negative subgroup age correlations in TCGA-BRCA; asterisks indicate significant ER-heterogeneity after FDR correction. (D) Per-gene matrix of adjusted discovery significance, directional external replication, ER heterogeneity, and reported study evidence classes. The displayed genes are a selected subset of the discovery results. A2M is Class B because of directional instability, despite nonsignificant ER heterogeneity. EGFR shows significant ER heterogeneity but does not meet the external-replication criterion. None of these associations establishes prognostic, therapeutic or clinical biomarker validity.

DATA PROVENANCE: ageaware-breast-cancer-final.zip / data/source_tables/Figure2_candidate_statistics.csv. The code in regenerate_figure2.py regenerates every submitted panel and checks key class- and sign-related conditions. No patient-level rerun or reanalysis was performed. External GSE42568 results are unadjusted Spearman correlations and therefore not estimand-equivalent to adjusted TCGA-BRCA coefficients.
